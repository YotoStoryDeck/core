🏠 Local Network (LAN) & Babysitter Guest Access

When enabled, devices on your private home Wi-Fi / RFC1918 LAN can open the Parent Portal (/) and Wall Kiosk (/kiosk) with member access without signing in. The Admin Console (/admin) and all remote WAN traffic always enforce authentication.
